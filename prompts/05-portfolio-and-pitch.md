# Prompt 5 - Portfolio page and pitch messages

## Portfolio

```
Build a one-page portfolio for me, [MY NAME], in [CITY]. I make simple websites and WhatsApp order pages for small businesses.

Include: a one-line statement of what I do, three sample cards (title, one sentence, link) for these links: [LINK 1], [LINK 2], [LINK 3], a "What you get" list, a "How it works in 3 steps" section, and a big WhatsApp button to [PHONE WITH COUNTRY CODE, NO +].

Do not invent clients, reviews, numbers or years of experience. One complete index.html, no external libraries, mobile-first, light on data.
```

## Pitch messages

```
Write 3 short WhatsApp messages (under 60 words each) in [LANGUAGE] that I can send to a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] owner I have never met. Polite, specific, no hype, no false claims. Each should: say who I am, give the sample link [LINK], make the offer "I build yours first, you pay only if you like it", and end with one easy question. Also write a 20-second version I can say in person.
```

## Etiquette

- One message, one follow-up three days later, then stop.
- Do not mass-message strangers or post the same advert in many groups; that gets your number banned.
