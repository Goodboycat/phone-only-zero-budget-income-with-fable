# 3. Step-by-step 30-day plan

About 1-2 hours a day. Tick each box. If a day takes two days, that is fine - do not skip steps.

## Week 1 - Set up and make your first sample

- [ ] **Day 1.** Create the free accounts in [01-phone-setup.md](01-phone-setup.md). Turn on two-factor login.
- [ ] **Day 2.** Walk around your area or scroll local Facebook/WhatsApp groups. Write down 20 small businesses with no website. Note the type that appears most (barber, food seller, tutor...). That is your niche.
- [ ] **Day 3.** Paste `prompts/01-local-business-site.md` into Fable for that niche. Get one complete `index.html`.
- [ ] **Day 4.** Publish it on GitHub Pages. Open the link, test every button. Ask Fable to fix anything broken (paste the exact problem).
- [ ] **Day 5.** Make a second sample in a different style, using the same prompt with different colours.
- [ ] **Day 6.** Paste `prompts/04-whatsapp-order-page.md` into Fable, or reuse `examples/whatsapp-order-page/`. Publish it as a third sample.
- [ ] **Day 7.** Ask Fable for a one-page portfolio listing your three samples and your WhatsApp number (`prompts/05-portfolio-and-pitch.md`). Publish it. This link is your business card.

## Week 2 - Talk to real people

- [ ] **Day 8.** Ask Fable for a short pitch message in your language (same prompt file). Keep it under 60 words.
- [ ] **Day 9-11.** Contact 10 businesses per day, in person or by WhatsApp. Show the sample on your phone. Offer: *"I will build yours first. You pay only if you like it."*
- [ ] **Day 12.** For everyone who said yes or maybe: collect business name, services, prices, hours, location, 3-6 photos, WhatsApp number.
- [ ] **Day 13.** Build their sites: tell Fable *"Replace the sample details with these real details"* and paste the details. Publish each in its own repository.
- [ ] **Day 14.** Deliver the links. Ask for payment (see [04-getting-paid.md](04-getting-paid.md)). Ask each happy client for one sentence of praise and one referral.

Expect most people to say no. Thirty conversations giving one to three paying clients is a normal result.

## Week 3 - Add a product that sells without you

- [ ] **Day 15.** Choose one audience you know well (students, job seekers, shop owners).
- [ ] **Day 16-17.** Use `prompts/02-digital-templates.md` to make a bundle of 5 templates. Save each as PDF from Chrome.
- [ ] **Day 18.** Create the product on Gumroad/Ko-fi/Selar. Ask Fable for the title, description and 5 FAQ answers. Set a low price or pay-what-you-want.
- [ ] **Day 19.** Give the bundle free to 5 people in exchange for honest feedback. Fix what confused them.
- [ ] **Day 20-21.** Post it where the audience is. Show the template being used, not just a cover image. One post per day, no spamming groups.

## Week 4 - Repeat what worked, drop what did not

- [ ] **Day 22.** Count: conversations, yes answers, money received, product views, sales. Write the numbers down.
- [ ] **Day 23-25.** Whichever of the two (client sites or templates) produced more results - do only that for three days.
- [ ] **Day 26.** Use `prompts/03-micro-tool.md` to build one free tool for the same audience. Put your product link and WhatsApp number at the bottom. Free tools bring visitors to paid things.
- [ ] **Day 27.** Offer existing clients a small monthly update plan (new prices, new photos, holiday notices).
- [ ] **Day 28.** Raise your price for new clients a little. You now have real examples.
- [ ] **Day 29.** Ask every client for a referral again.
- [ ] **Day 30.** Review the numbers. Decide the next 30 days: more of the same niche, or one new niche.

## If you earned nothing in 30 days

Check in this order:
1. **Did you really talk to 30+ people?** Most failures are here.
2. **Was the sample good on their phone?** Ask three strangers what they think it is for.
3. **Wrong niche?** Pick businesses that already take orders by phone/WhatsApp - they feel the benefit fastest.
4. **Price?** Ask people who said no what price would have been a yes.

## Working with Fable efficiently

- Ask for **one complete file** each time, not pieces.
- When something breaks, describe exactly: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened.
- Say **"must work on a small Android screen, low data, no external libraries"** in every build request.
- Keep the final working version in GitHub before asking for changes, so you can go back.
