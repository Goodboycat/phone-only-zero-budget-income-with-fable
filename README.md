# Phone-Only, Zero-Budget Income with Fable

**Question this repo answers:**
> What can I ask Fable to build so I can earn money without spending a single penny, using only an Android phone (P65) and no PC?

## Read this first (honest expectations)

- **Nothing here is guaranteed money.** There is no button that prints cash. What an AI builder can do is make *things people pay for* (tools, templates, pages, small games) very quickly. You still have to put them in front of people.
- **Realistic timeline:** first small payment in 2-8 weeks if you work 1-2 hours a day. Many people earn $0 in the first month. That is normal.
- **Your real costs are time and mobile data**, not money. Everything in this repo uses free tiers only.
- **Avoid anything that looks like "winning" money**: betting bots, casino predictors, crypto signal apps, "auto-earning" apps. They lose money or are scams. See [docs/05-scams-to-avoid.md](docs/05-scams-to-avoid.md).

## Assumptions

- "Fable" = the AI assistant/builder you chat with, which can write code, text and designs for you. The prompts work with any capable AI builder.
- "P65" = a budget Android phone such as the itel P65 (small RAM, modest screen). Everything here runs in the Chrome browser, so the exact model does not matter.

## The short answer

Ask Fable to build **small, single-file web products** that you can host for free and sell or monetise from your phone:

| # | What to ask Fable to build | How it earns | Difficulty | Speed to first money |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Websites for local businesses** (barber, restaurant, tutor) | They pay you a one-time fee | Easy | Fastest |
| 2 | **Digital templates** (CV, invoice, budget planner, study planner) | Sell on Gumroad / Ko-fi / Selar | Easy | Medium |
| 3 | **Micro web tools** (calculators, converters, generators) | Tips, affiliate links, later ads | Easy | Slow |
| 4 | **WhatsApp/Telegram business helpers** (menu page, order form, price list) | Setup fee from small shops | Medium | Fast |
| 5 | **Small browser games** | Itch.io pay-what-you-want | Medium | Slow |
| 6 | **Niche content site** (guides in your language) | Affiliate links, ads later | Medium | Slowest |

**Recommended path: start with #1, add #2 in parallel.** Idea #1 gets paid by a real person you can talk to; #2 keeps selling while you sleep.
