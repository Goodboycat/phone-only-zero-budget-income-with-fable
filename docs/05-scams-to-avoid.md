# 5. Scams and dead ends to avoid

Searching for "win money with no money on my phone" attracts scammers. Assume any offer that finds *you* is one.

## Do not build or join these

| Thing | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| Betting / casino / "aviator" predictor apps | The games are random or controlled by the house. Predictors cannot work; sellers earn from you. |
| Crypto or forex trading bots, signal groups | Without capital you have nothing to trade; with capital most beginners lose it. |
| "Watch ads / tap to earn / install apps" schemes | Pay cents per hour, often never pay out, and harvest your data. |
| Pyramid or referral-only schemes | You earn only by recruiting others who pay. Illegal in many places. |
| "Pay a small fee to unlock your earnings" | Classic advance-fee fraud. |
| Fake reviews, fake followers, account farming | Breaks platform rules; accounts get banned and it can be illegal. |
| Reselling copied courses, movies, apps | Copyright infringement; stores remove you and keep the balance. |
| Receiving money "for someone else" and forwarding it | That is money laundering (being a money mule), a crime. |

## Warning signs

- Guaranteed or fixed daily returns.
- Pressure to act now.
- Screenshots of payments as proof.
- Someone asks for your OTP, PIN, ID photo, or remote access to your phone.
- You have to pay before you can earn or withdraw.

## What real earning looks like

Someone has a problem, you solve it, they pay you. Every idea in this repo follows that shape. It is slower than the promises above, and it is the only kind that works.
