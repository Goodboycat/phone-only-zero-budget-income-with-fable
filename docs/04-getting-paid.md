# 4. Getting paid with no upfront cost

## From local clients (Ideas 1 and 4)

- Use what people around you already use: **mobile money, bank transfer, or cash**. No platform needed.
- Agree the price in writing on WhatsApp before you start: what they get, the price, when they pay.
- Safe structure for beginners: build first, show it, get paid on approval. Later, ask 50% before starting.
- Send a simple receipt (use `examples/invoice-generator/`).

## From online sales (Ideas 2, 3, 5)

| Platform | Cost to start | How they charge | Check before using |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | Free | Percentage of each sale | Which payout methods reach your country |
| Ko-fi | Free | Free tier available; payment processor fees apply | Needs PayPal or Stripe in your country |
| Selar / Paystack storefront / Flutterwave store | Free | Percentage per sale | Good for mobile money and local cards in several African countries |
| Itch.io | Free | You choose their share | Payout options |

Fees and supported countries change. **Read each platform's own pricing and payout page before you choose** - do not rely on this table.

## Rules that keep you safe

- You never need to pay to receive money. Anyone asking for an "activation" or "withdrawal" fee is scamming you.
- Do not share one-time codes (OTP), PINs or passwords with clients or "support agents".
- Keep a simple record of every payment: date, who, how much, what for. A notes app is enough.
- Income may be taxable where you live. Once it is regular, ask a local tax office or a trusted adult what applies.
- If you are under 18, platforms usually require a parent or guardian to own the payout account.

## Pricing guidance

- Do not copy prices from other countries. Ask: what does this business pay for a printed banner or a month of flyers? Price a website near that.
- First three clients: low price in exchange for a testimonial and permission to show the site as a sample.
- Templates: low single price, higher bundle price. Bundles usually bring more total income.
